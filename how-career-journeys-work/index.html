What is a career journey path?

A career journey path is like Google Maps for your career — not a one-time counselling session, but a structured route from Class 11 or 12 to career entry, with milestones, services, and decisions documented along the way.

How it works

  1. 1

    Search

    Tell us the career you want — e.g. doctor, engineer, designer.

  2. 2

    Share your profile

    Location, school, stream, academics, and family earning help us personalise.

  3. 3

    Get 3–4 paths

    Merrakii+ prepares primary and alternative routes matched to your context.

  4. 4

    Follow the map

    Open the full path, services, and counsellor support on your journey map.
