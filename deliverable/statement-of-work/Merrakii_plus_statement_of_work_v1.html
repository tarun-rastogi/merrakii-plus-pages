1. Parties & purpose

This Statement of Work (“SOW”) is between Maple AI Technologies (“Maple AI”) and Munjal Universal Consultancy Private Limited, operating as Merrakii+ (“Merrakii”). It references Merrakii+ Product Requirement Document v1.0, July 2026 (“PRD”). Commercial fees are in the companion Quotation (MAT_QTN_2026_002).

Phase 1 delivers one responsive web application (320px through desktop; browser-based — not native mobile apps) for the Horizon 1 MVP. PRD metrics M1–M10 are Merrakii’s post-launch business KPIs, not Maple AI development acceptance criteria.

Merrakii+ is a Decision Enabling Student Success System (DESSS) — a five-stage career guidance platform for Class 9–12 students and families, delivered through counsellors, career professionals, and partner counsellors, with the Career Journey Map as the flagship experience.

4. Integrations & hosting

Boundary. Maple AI integrates and deploys using credentials Merrakii supplies. Merrakii owns all vendor contracts, merchant onboarding/KYC, AWS account billing, and usage fees.

Phase 1 — Maple AI engineering (finalized at Design sign-off):

  • Transactional email; Google Meet; calendar scheduling (default Google Calendar); Razorpay checkout and webhooks for enrolled tier; session workflows in counsellor and professional portals.
  • AWS architecture, staging/production setup, deployment pipeline, monitoring baseline, and AWS runbook — on Merrakii’s AWS account from Development through go-live.

Phase 2 or change order: WhatsApp Business API, SMS, CRM sync (F08), analytics dashboards (F09).

5. Out of scope

Unless added by written change order:

  • P1 features F07, F08, F09; native mobile apps.
  • Vendor procurement, payment gateway KYC, AWS usage charges, domain/DNS/SSL fees.
  • Full journey library content authorship, legal drafting, and formal penetration-test certification (Merrakii).

8. Acceptance criteria

PRD M1–M10 are Merrakii business targets after launch — not payment or sign-off conditions for Maple AI.
  1. All Section 3 features, Section 4 integrations and AWS hosting, demonstrable on AWS staging/production.
  2. Section 6 standards met; no open Severity-1 defects at UAT; Severity-2 items documented with fix or workaround.
  3. End-to-end journey passes: browse → register → journey map → enrolled payment → counsellor session (calendar + Meet) → decision log → parent visibility.
  4. Handover pack: AWS runbook, environment and integration setup guides, content import guidance.

9. Assumptions, change control & terms

  • Merrakii provides structured content during Design; AWS account with IAM access and Phase 1 vendor credentials before Development.
  • Integration providers signed off at Design; changes require change order. Horizon 1 personalisation may be rules-based unless agreed otherwise.
  • Work outside this SOW — P1 features, extra integrations, native apps, or P0 scope changes — requires written change request and commercial adjustment via Quotation or change order.

Fees and payment schedule are exclusively in Quotation MAT_QTN_2026_002, tied to Section 7 milestones. This SOW with the Quotation and any change orders constitutes the Horizon 1 agreement. Either party may terminate for material breach with 15 days’ written notice and cure period. Both parties maintain confidentiality of the PRD and this SOW.
