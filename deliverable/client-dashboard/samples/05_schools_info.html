The Problem Your School Is Navigating

Four pressures, one root cause

None of these are your school's fault individually. Together, they describe a system gap no single institution can close alone.

NEP 2020 mandates career guidance
The National Education Policy requires structured career guidance from Class 9 for every student. Most schools have neither the system nor the bandwidth to deliver it at the required depth.
One counsellor. Hundreds of students.
The average school counsellor manages 300 to 500 students with no structured framework, no validated career content, and no parent engagement system. Depth is impossible at that ratio.
Parents expect guidance. Schools lack the infrastructure.
Increasingly, parents hold schools accountable for career outcomes. When students make wrong decisions, parents remember where the counselling came from. Schools need a credible, documented system behind them.
Wrong decisions affect your reputation.
A student who chooses the wrong stream, fails at entrance preparation, or regrets their college — tells that story. Their parents tell it more. This shapes how your school is spoken about, long after the decision is made.

The Pilot — How We Begin

Six steps, zero cost, no long commitment

Designed so a principal can say yes without a procurement cycle — and see real outcomes before any larger decision.

1
Initial Conversation
45 minutes with the school principal or counselling head. We listen first — your students, your challenges, your goals. No pitch until we understand the school.
2
Pilot Proposal
We present a customised proposal — specific to your student profile, class composition, and school context. Zero cost to the school. Specific outcomes. Clear process.
3
Agreement and Cohort
A simple one-page agreement signed. A cohort of 25 to 50 students across Class 9 to 12 identified. Parent consent collected by the school.
4
Pilot Delivery
Merrakii+ counsellors deliver Discover and Explore stages over 6 to 8 weeks. Career professional sessions scheduled. Parent sessions run in parallel.
5
Outcomes Report
A complete report presented to school leadership — decisions enabled, stage completion, student and parent feedback, and recommendations for the full programme.
6
Full Partnership
If the pilot outcomes meet expectations, we move to a full school programme covering all eligible students with a structured annual delivery plan.
