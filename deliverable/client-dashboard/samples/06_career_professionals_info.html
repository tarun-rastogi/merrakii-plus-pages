You have built a career.Now help a student choose one.

Students in India make one of the most consequential decisions of their lives — choosing a career — without ever speaking to a single person who works in that career. Not because professionals are unwilling. Because no structured system exists to connect them. Merrakii+ is that system.

The most important thing you bring to Merrakii+ cannot be found in any content library, any AI model, or any counsellor's notes. It has a name.

It is called reality.
What You Do

Three ways your career becomes someone else's clarity

This isn't a one-off guest appearance. It's a standing role with three distinct forms of contribution.

VALIDATE

You review and confirm every career journey in your domain before it is published. Your professional experience is the quality mark — a journey published on Merrakii+ carries your name, and your credibility.

One validation review per journey — typically 30 minutes, at your convenience.

SPEAK

You conduct structured 45-minute sessions with students exploring your career. One honest conversation from you changes how a student sees their future — often more than anything else in their research.

Sessions are scheduled at your convenience. Merrakii+ handles preparation, format, and follow-up entirely.

SHAPE

Over time, your input shapes the career journey library for your entire domain — making every future student's decision better informed, long after any single conversation ends.

You are not a guest speaker. You are a permanent contributor to one of India's most important educational resources.
