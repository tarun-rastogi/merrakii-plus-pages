Our Philosophy

From Memory Mode to Intelligence Mode

Most students make career decisions reacting to whoever spoke last. Merrakii+ exists to change that — one decision at a time.

🌀 Memory Mode
  • Choosing a stream because a friend picked it too
  • Finding out about deadlines two weeks before they close
  • Following whichever advice was heard most recently
  • Panic-researching colleges the month before applications
🧭 Intelligence Mode
  • Choosing a stream because it's mapped to your actual profile
  • Knowing every deadline months in advance, with time to prepare
  • Weighing advice against your own information, not just volume
  • Walking into applications with a shortlist you built deliberately
