The Training Programme

You are trained as a professional, not a sales agent

Five tiers, building from foundation to specialisation — designed to respect your intelligence and the seriousness of what families are trusting you with.

01
Foundation Residential
5 days at the Merrakii+ corporate office. You experience the system as a student first — discovery, direction, decision. Then you learn to deliver it.
02
Online Certification
4 weeks self-paced. Career journeys, counselling conversation skills, family dynamics, platform tools, assessment interpretation.
03
Monthly Interaction
A monthly online session with the full Partner Counsellor network — new content, peer cases, continuous development.
04
Domain Specialisation
Choose your track — Science, Commerce, Creative, or Government careers. Deep expertise built for your community's actual needs.
05
Professional Development
Advanced family counselling, decision-making philosophy, ethics and boundaries. Reserved for the most experienced counsellors.
