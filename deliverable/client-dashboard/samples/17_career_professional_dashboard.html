How is my time commitment tracked?⌄
Every session and validation you complete is logged automatically once it's marked done — nothing to report manually. Your Overview tab always reflects your actual, current contribution.
Is this a paid role?⌄
Your core contribution — Validate, Speak, Shape — stays recognition-based, not paid. What you receive there is a verified public profile, a community of peers, and standing recognition, including at the annual Forum. Alongside that, you can also offer paid Premium Sessions (1-on-1 or group), which students or schools book and pay for directly — Merrakii+ handles billing and pays out your share, tracked in the Earnings tab. The two are separate and you're never required to take part in the paid track.
What if I need to reschedule a session?⌄
Use the reschedule option on any upcoming session in My Sessions — Merrakii+ handles notifying the student and finding a new slot. No need to coordinate this yourself.
