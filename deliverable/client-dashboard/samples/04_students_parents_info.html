What Merrakii+ Does — For You

Built for two people, not one

A career decision is never just the student's, and it's never just the parent's either. Merrakii+ is built for the conversation between you.

🎓 For the Student
You discover who you are before you choose what to become
A structured discovery process maps your genuine interests, personality, and strengths — not what the class topper chose, not what your relatives suggest, not what the coaching institute is selling.
You see the complete picture of every career you consider
Every career journey shows the complete route — academic stream, entrance exams, institutions, skills required, investment needed, and what the career actually pays. Validated by a professional who works in that career.
You speak to someone who actually works in your chosen career
Before your family commits to any direction, you have a structured 45-minute conversation with a working professional in that career. Reality — not assumption — drives your decision.
👨‍👩‍👧 For the Parent
You understand exactly what you are committing to
For every career your child considers, you see a complete financial map — government vs. private college, total fees over the entire degree, coaching costs, and expected starting salary. No surprises after the commitment.
You are present at every stage — not just at the end
Parent sessions are built into every stage of the journey. You don't find out what your child has decided — you're part of how they decide. Family alignment is a documented output, not an assumption.
You get professional guidance — not just a counsellor's opinion
Your family's most significant investment decision is guided by career professionals, validated career data, and a trained counsellor working with you — not selling to you.
