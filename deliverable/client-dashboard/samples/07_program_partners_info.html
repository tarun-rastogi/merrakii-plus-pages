Why the Merrakii+ Channel Is Different

Not another lead-gen funnel

The difference isn't traffic volume. It's what's already true about the student before your programme ever appears.

Every Other Channel
  • Students who see your programme are browsing — at best interested, at worst distracted
  • No relationship between student need and programme offering — the match is based on demographics, not decision stage
  • Parents are not in the room. The student enquires. The parent vetoes. The sale fails.
  • No counsellor context. No validated career direction. No accountability for outcome.
The Merrakii+ Channel
  • Students see your programme at Stage 3 — Develop — when they have a confirmed direction and a counsellor has told them this is the next step
  • The match is precise. A NEET-track student in Class 11 sees Biology and Chemistry preparation. A Finance-track student sees CA Foundation coaching.
  • The parent has already attended an alignment session. The family has seen the financial map. The enrolment decision is pre-validated.
  • The counsellor recommends. The student trusts the recommendation. Conversion is a natural next step — not a sales event.
