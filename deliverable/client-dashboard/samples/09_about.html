About Merrakii+

Career decisions shouldn't be guesses.

Merrakii+ is a Decision Enabling Student Success System — built because most students in India choose a career the way they choose most things at sixteen: reacting to whoever spoke last, not deciding from a place of genuine understanding.

Our Story

Why this exists

Every year, millions of Indian families make one of the most consequential financial and life decisions they will ever face — choosing a career for their child — with almost no structured support. Schools are stretched thin. Coaching institutes have their own programme to sell. Well-meaning relatives repeat what worked for them, whether or not it fits.

Merrakii+ was built to close that gap — not by replacing the people already trying to help (schools, counsellors, working professionals), but by giving all of them a shared, structured system: a real journey map from Class 9 to Career Entry, real conversations with people who actually work in the field, and real documentation of every decision made along the way.

We're based in Gurugram, Haryana, and built by Munjal Universal Consultancy Private Limited.

Our Philosophy

From Memory Mode to Intelligence Mode

The idea underneath every product decision we make.

🌀 Memory Mode
Reacting to whoever spoke last. Choosing a stream because a friend did. Panic-researching colleges the month before applications close.
🧭 Intelligence Mode
Responding from a plan built on your own profile. Understanding why a decision fits you. Knowing your timeline months in advance.
