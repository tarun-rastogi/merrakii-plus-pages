Product north star

From Product Design: Merrakii is not “just another career site.” It is a Decision Intelligence system — one connected destination for academic and career choices.

Vision

Build the world’s most comprehensive Decision Intelligence System that empowers every student to make informed academic and career decisions through AI, validated knowledge, immersive experiences, and continuous guidance.

Philosophy

Every student starts their journey differently, but every student deserves one connected destination. Students think in aspirations, degrees, colleges, exams, skills, or professions — Merrakii unifies these into one intelligent decision ecosystem.

“One Platform. Every Student Decision.”
By Intelligence Student, academic, career, institution, examination, industry & decision intelligence working together.
By Entry Points Start from career, degree, college, exam, stream, skills, industry, or study abroad — then connect to the full journey.
By Experience Personalized, immersive, guided, and collaborative — students, parents, counsellors, partners, professionals.
By Outcome Clarity, confidence, direction, readiness, personalized roadmap, continuous progress.

How Maple AI will deliver

We ship in levels: first a clickable student core path, then enrolment depth, then content & legal pages, then admin CMS layers, and finally platform foundations. Each level is usable and reviewable on its own.

01 — Vertical slices

Ship journeys, not orphan screens

Levels 1–2 follow the real student path: search → profile → journey → pay → dashboard → courses. That keeps demos honest and feedback tied to outcomes.

02 — Design system + scope

Product Design guides UX; workbook locks scope

Experience principles come from Product Design. Page inventory and Phase 1 boundaries come from Merrakii_pages_inscope.xlsx — so build order stays aligned with client priorities.

03 — Harden last

CMS and platform after the student loop works

Admin CMS (Levels 4–5) and non-UI foundations (Level 6) land once student flows are stable — so content ops and reliability support a proven journey, not a moving target.

Clarity for stakeholders Reviewable milestones Reduced rework Production-ready increments
