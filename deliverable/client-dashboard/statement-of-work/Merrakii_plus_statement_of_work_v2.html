This Statement of Work V2 (“SOW”) sets out the Phase 1 development scope for Merrakii+: the crux of the application, the agreed pages, and what is out of scope. SOW V1 remains on file as an archived baseline.

1. Parties and purpose

This SOW is between Maple AI Technologies (“Maple AI”) and Munjal Universal Consultancy Private Limited, operating as Merrakii+ (“Merrakii”).

Maple AI will design, build, and ship Phase 1 of Merrakii+ as one responsive web application (usable in a browser on mobile, tablet, and desktop). This engagement is product engineering: Maple AI ships the agreed application. It is not staff augmentation, an embedded engineering pod, or standalone IT consultation.

Merrakii owns product content, page copy, business requirements for each page, vendor accounts, payment KYC, and AWS usage. Maple AI owns architecture, implementation of the agreed pages and supporting platform, UAT support, and handover.

2. Scope of development

This section is the contracted development scope for Phase 1.

2.1 Crux of the application

Maple AI will develop Merrakii+ as a student-goal web product. A student goal may be a career, a degree, a skill, a college, or an exam. A student can search for a goal, get a path to achieve it, pay to personalize that path and start moving on it, and then be guided throughout the journey to services that help them move forward and achieve the goal. The student can pay for those services to avail them and keep moving on the path. Merrakii staff will operate the product from an admin console. The crux of what will be built is:

  • A public website where a student can search and enter a journey toward a chosen goal (career, degree, skill, college, or exam).
  • A journey map that gives the student a path to achieve the selected goal.
  • Deep profiling and journey payment so the student can pay to personalize that path and start moving on it.
  • A student dashboard where, throughout the journey, the application guides the student: overview, current path, journey report, notifications, and the services and resources that help them move forward and achieve the goal.
  • Service and course pages so the student can see a recommended service, enrol or avail it, and pay for that service to move forward on the path.
  • Public trust and information pages: About Merrakii, Contact Us, FAQs, Terms and Conditions, Privacy Policy, and Refund Policy.
  • An admin console so Merrakii can manage the goal library (career, degree, skill, college, and exam), students, service knowledge, courses, resources, and a financials overview.
  • Payment integration for path personalization and for services the student avails along the journey.
  • The website architecture, access and security, search, data, workflows, testing, AWS production deployment, and handover needed to run the pages above.

This Phase 1 product is a browser-based web application only.

2.3 Functionalities on the agreed pages

The contracted commitment is to build the agreed pages listed in Section 2.2.

The functionalities placed on those pages must fulfil the business requirements for each page. Merrakii will give those business requirements, page by page.

Maple AI will implement each agreed page so that it meets the business requirements given for that page, within the Phase 1 product described in Section 2.1. Requirements that add a new page, a new user role, a new dashboard, scheduling, a native app, or AI are outside this SOW and need a written change order.

3. Out of scope

The following are not part of this development, unless added later by a written change order:

  • Counsellor meeting scheduling
  • Professional expert meeting scheduling
  • Counsellor dashboard
  • Professional expert dashboard
  • Parent dashboard
  • Vendor dashboard
  • School dashboard
  • Mobile applications (native iOS or Android apps)
  • AI features

4. Client inputs required

Merrakii will provide, in time for the relevant page build:

  • Business requirements for each agreed page (Section 2.3).
  • Page copy and legal text for About, Contact, FAQs, Terms, Privacy, and Refund.
  • Goal, service, course, and resource content or templates needed to populate the agreed pages (a goal may be a career, a degree, a skill, a college, or an exam).
  • Payment gateway account details and KYC completion for payment integration.
  • AWS account access for staging and production.

Where an input is late, Maple AI may ship the page with a documented placeholder and complete the content when the input arrives.

5. Acceptance

Phase 1 is accepted when:

  • Each agreed page in Section 2.2 is demonstrable on staging, and on production at go-live.
  • Functionalities on those pages meet the business requirements that were given for that page in time for the build.
  • No open Severity-1 defects remain for in-scope pages at UAT sign-off. Severity-2 items are listed with a fix plan or workaround.
  • Maple AI has handed over the runbook, environment notes, and admin guidance needed to operate the agreed admin pages.

6. Change control

Work outside Sections 2 and 3 of this SOW, including any item listed in Section 3, requires a written change request and a commercial adjustment.

This SOW V2, together with any change orders, governs Phase 1 delivery.
