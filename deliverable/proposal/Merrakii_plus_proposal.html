Project

Merrakii+ Decision Enabling Student Success System (DESSS), web platform development

1. Executive summary

This proposal from Maple AI Technologies covers the complete design and development of the Merrakii+ Decision Enabling Student Success System, as described in the Product Requirement Document version 1.0 shared by the Merrakii+ team in July 2026.

Merrakii+ is a career guidance platform that works with students of Class 9 to Class 12 and with their families at the same time. The heart of the platform is the Career Journey Map. A student enters their class, career goal, academic performance, interests, and family background, and the platform creates a personal, visual roadmap from today to career entry. Trained counsellors, working career professionals, and a network of partner counsellors then guide the student and the family along that map, stage by stage, until a real, documented career decision is made.

The platform serves nine different types of users, offers a library of validated career journeys, a catalogue of 32 guidance services, separate dashboards for each type of user, and built-in intelligence that personalises every student's journey. The total professional fee for the web platform is Rupees fifteen lakh (INR 15,00,000), delivered over nine weeks across five clear milestones, with payment linked to the completion of each milestone.

3. Features for the student

The student is at the centre of the platform. A student who creates a free profile receives the following.

  • A personal Career Journey Map. The student's own visual roadmap from their current class to career entry, showing every stage, every milestone, and every service along the way, in the style of a route on a map.
  • Self-discovery assessments. Structured assessments that help the student understand their own interests, personality, and strengths before any career direction is chosen.
  • The full career journey library. Complete, professionally verified information for each career: the academic path, entrance exams, colleges, skills, total cost through government and private routes, scholarships, and realistic salary expectations.
  • Sessions with real professionals. Structured 45 minute conversations with a verified professional who actually works in the career the student is exploring.
  • A progress tracker. A clear view of how far the student has come, what percentage of the journey is complete, what the next milestone is, and when the next session is scheduled.
  • Alternative routes. If the primary route does not work out, the map already shows route B and route C, so no setback becomes a dead end.
  • A fast track for late joiners. A student who joins in Class 11 or Class 12 completes a compressed discovery session and is placed at exactly the right point on the map.
  • A documented decision. The journey leads to a real career decision that the student, the parents, and the counsellor confirm together, recorded on the platform.
  • Enrolled student benefits. Students who enrol additionally receive their assigned counsellor, all 32 services, a document vault, full session history, and the parent dashboard for their family.

6. AI integration features

Intelligence is built into the platform where it genuinely helps the student and the family. Each feature below has a clear, practical purpose.

  • Personalised journey generation. The platform takes the student's class, career goal, academic performance, interests, and family income range and generates a career roadmap tailored to that exact student, including the correct starting milestone for late joiners.
  • Plain language career search. The search bar on the home page understands everyday questions such as "how do I become a doctor" or "careers for someone who loves drawing" and returns the right journeys.
  • Journey matching after discovery. Once a student completes the discovery assessments, the platform suggests the three to five career journeys that best fit their profile, so counselling time is spent on the strongest options.
  • Smart related suggestions. Every journey page suggests adjacent careers, so a student exploring medicine also discovers allied fields they may never have considered.
  • Session preparation summaries. Before each session, the counsellor receives a short, automatically prepared summary of the student's progress, recent notes, and suggested talking points, saving preparation time across a large caseload.
  • Early warning signals. The platform watches for students who are slowing down or disengaging and flags them to their counsellor before a small drift becomes a dropout.

10. Compliance

The platform serves school students, most of whom are minors, so compliance is treated as a core requirement and not an afterthought. The following commitments are built into the delivery.

  • Protection of personal data. The platform will be built in line with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. Personal data is collected only with clear consent, used only for the stated purpose, and never sold or shared with third parties beyond the integrations listed in this proposal.
  • Care for minors' data. Student accounts are designed with the linked parent account in mind, so a parent or guardian stays informed and involved. Data belonging to minors receives the strictest handling on the platform.
  • Role based access at every level. Every user sees only what their role permits. A counsellor sees only their own caseload, a school sees only aggregate cohort numbers, and a professional sees only the students booked with them.
  • Secure engineering practices. All traffic is encrypted, passwords are stored using strong one way hashing, and payment details are handled entirely by the payment gateway and never stored on the platform.
  • Security review before launch. A structured security audit is completed before go live, covering authentication, access control, and data handling, in line with the success criteria in the requirement document.
  • Accessibility. All public pages are built to meet the WCAG 2.1 AA accessibility standard, so the platform is usable by people with disabilities.
  • Performance and availability. Pages are engineered to load within three seconds on a 4G mobile connection, the platform works fully on screens as small as 320 pixels wide, and availability of 99.5 percent during business hours is the operating target, with monitoring and a downtime log in place.
  • Auditability. Every decision recorded on the platform carries the confirmation of the student, the parent, and the counsellor, creating a clean, reviewable trail for the programme's most important metric.
