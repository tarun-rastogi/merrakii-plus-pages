We measure one thing: decisions enabled.

Every year, millions of students in India make one of the most consequential decisions of their lives — choosing a stream, a college, a career — without a single structured, informed conversation. Not because they don't deserve one. Because no system has ever reached them.

Merrakii+ is that system. Not a course marketplace. Not a counselling directory. A Decision Enabling Student Success System — working simultaneously with students and their families, from Class 9 to career entry.

Our philosophy

Self-knowledge before selection

A career decision made from internal clarity outlasts one made from external pressure. Every journey starts with structured self-discovery.

The family decides together

In India, career decisions are family decisions. We are the only platform that aligns student and parents before anything is chosen.

Reach is the mission

Through the Partner Counsellor Network, Merrakii+ reaches families at the doorstep — students no school contract and no app store can reach.
